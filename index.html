How to install on AndroidAndroidga qanday oʻrnatiladiКак установить на Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open in the notification, or find TrackMe.apk in your Files → Downloads.
  3. Android may say installing apps from this source isn't allowed. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open TrackMe and sign in with Google or your email.
Why the warnings? TrackMe isn't on Google Play yet, so Android doesn't know the developer. The app asks for no special permissions: no camera, contacts, location or files.

Problems?Muammo boʻlsa?Проблемы?

  • "App not installed": an older test version may be in the way. Uninstall TrackMe first, then install again. Your data is safe in your account.
  • "There was a problem parsing the package": the download didn't finish, or your phone runs Android older than 7.0. Download again.
  • Sign-in doesn't open: make sure Google Chrome is installed and updated.
  • "Ilova oʻrnatilmadi": eski sinov versiyasi xalaqit berayotgan boʻlishi mumkin. Avval TrackMe ni oʻchiring, keyin qayta oʻrnating. Maʼlumotlaringiz hisobingizda saqlanadi.
  • "Paketni tahlil qilishda xato": yuklab olish tugamagan yoki telefoningizda Android 7.0 dan eski versiya. Qayta yuklab oling.
  • Kirish oynasi ochilmayapti: Google Chrome oʻrnatilgan va yangilangan boʻlishi kerak.
  • «Приложение не установлено»: мешает старая тестовая версия. Удалите TrackMe и установите снова. Данные сохранены в аккаунте.
  • «Ошибка при синтаксическом анализе пакета»: файл скачался не полностью или Android на телефоне старше 7.0. Скачайте заново.
  • Не открывается вход: проверьте, что Google Chrome установлен и обновлён.
